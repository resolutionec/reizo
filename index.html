Design and Manufacture with our Team

Full Service Eyewear Design

Reizo is a professional OEM/ODM eyewear manufacturer. We have an experienced design team that specializes in design and product prototyping. Our commitment is to design every product to meet customer requirements. We also modify designs based on customer drawings or customize it to spec and scale based on client designs. We provide a one-stop service from design to manufacturing.

Quality Control

We have a complete ISO quality control system, from raw material inspection, accessories inspection and testing, production process inspection, finished product appearance inspection and function testing, etc., strictly arrange ISO quality control requirements to ensure that customers receive the most satisfactory products.

Quality control image

Stress Test Process

We possess a range of eyewear testing machines, including eyeglass frame fatigue testing machines, eyeglass hinge opening and closing testing machines, sun-lens stress testing machines, Polaroid lens testing machines, sun-lens drop ball testing machines, metal and titanium eyeglass frame salt spray testing machines, sweat testing machines, eyeglass frame reading machines, eyeglass frame strength testing machines, etc. All our new products, new structures, and new materials undergo rigorous testing to ensure that the final product meets customer requirements.

Certificates & Compliance

Our factory has undergone rigorous ISO9001–ISO45001 certification, as well as FDA, ISCC, CE and other certifications. We strictly follow the requirements of these certifications during the production process to ensure the production of high-quality products.

Are your frames authentic?

Yes. Reizo sources every frame through authorised reseller accounts, direct from the makers.

02

Do the frames take prescription lenses?

Yes. Optical frames such as Model M3171 ship with clear demo lenses, ready for your optician to glaze with your prescription.

03

Are the colorways on this site exact?

The 3D colorways are concept renders. The sample photographs on each product page show the real finishes.

04

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. Opticians and boutiques can open a wholesale account. Choose “Wholesale” in the enquiry form.

05

Can I order a frame that isn't shown here?

Often, yes. Send the model number and color and we will check availability with the maker.

06

How long does delivery take?

It depends on stock and destination. We confirm the timeline when we reply to your enquiry.

07

What about warranty and repairs?

Warranty claims and replacement parts are handled through Reizo with the maker. Keep your order details and get in touch.

08

How do I start an enquiry?

Use the enquiry form on the Contact page, or email info@reizo.example with the models you have in mind and your quantities.
